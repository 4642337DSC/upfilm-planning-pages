Area of expertise

Blown-in insulation materials (Isofiber cellulose, Isofulg basalt wool, imported wood fiber). Positions installation quality as equally important as thermal coefficient; offers an online sizing calculator and technical explainer content.

Homeowners/self-builders and contractors building nZEB or passive houses, plus architects/consultants. Technically curious, price-comparing insulation options.

Romanian, "noi", expert/technical-explainer register. Myth-busting hooks, precise numbers, health/comfort framing over pure energy-bill savings.
